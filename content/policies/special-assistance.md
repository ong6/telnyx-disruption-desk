# Nimbus Air Special Assistance Policy

This is the special assistance policy for Nimbus Air, code N B.

Special assistance is always handled by a specialist agent. This includes wheelchair support, medical assistance, and travel for unaccompanied minors.

Requests must be made at least 48 hours before departure whenever possible. Contact Nimbus Air customer service or visit any Nimbus Air ticket office.

Wheelchair assistance is free. It covers the journey from the terminal entrance, through check-in and security, to your seat, and again on arrival. Wheelchairs are available at all Nimbus Air airports.

For medical assistance, tell us about any condition that requires oxygen, a stretcher, or medical clearance. A fit-to-fly certificate from your doctor may be required within 10 days before travel.

Unaccompanied minors must be between 5 and 15 years old. The service includes dedicated boarding, in-flight supervision, and handover to a named guardian at arrival. The fee is 75 Singapore dollars per flight segment. Guardians must stay at the airport until 30 minutes after departure.

Requests are confirmed within 24 hours by a specialist agent.
