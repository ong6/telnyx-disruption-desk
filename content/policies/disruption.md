# Nimbus Air Disruption Policy

This is the disruption policy for Nimbus Air, code N B.

Typhoon Koinu waiver for Hong Kong. Due to Typhoon Koinu, flights to and from Hong Kong are affected. Under this waiver, you may change your booking free of charge with no fare difference. Changed travel must be completed within 7 days of your original departure date. You may also request a refund to your original payment method. The waiver applies to tickets issued before the waiver start date for travel to or from Hong Kong.

If your Nimbus Air flight is cancelled or delayed overnight, we will provide a hotel stay and meals. Stranded passengers receive a hotel and meals voucher worth up to 150 Singapore dollars per person per night. Vouchers can be issued over the phone or at the airport service desk.

For delays under 3 hours, monitor the Nimbus Air app for updates. For delays over 3 hours, visit the service desk for meal vouchers and rebooking options.

During major disruptions, rebooking onto the next available Nimbus Air flight is always free of charge.
