# Nimbus Air Baggage Policy

This is the baggage policy for Nimbus Air, code N B, hub Singapore.

Every economy passenger may check in one bag weighing up to 30 kilograms. The bag must not exceed total linear dimensions of 158 centimetres. Oversized or overweight bags are accepted at the airport for a fee of 75 Singapore dollars per piece.

In the cabin, you may bring one personal item and one carry-on bag with a combined weight of 7 kilograms. The carry-on must fit in the overhead bin, measuring no more than 55 by 35 by 20 centimetres. Personal items include a handbag, laptop bag, or small backpack that fits under the seat.

Lithium batteries and power banks must travel in the cabin, never in checked baggage. Each power bank is limited to 100 watt hours.

Arrive at least 2 hours before departure for baggage drop. Counters close 45 minutes before departure for Singapore flights and 60 minutes for international flights.

If your checked bag is delayed or damaged, report it at the baggage desk within 7 days.
