# Nimbus Air Refund Policy

This is the refund policy for Nimbus Air, code N B.

If Nimbus Air cancels your flight, you may rebook free of charge onto any Nimbus Air flight on the same route within 7 days of the original departure. Alternatively, you may take a full refund to your original payment method. Refunds are processed within 7 business days and include all taxes and fees.

If you cancel your booking yourself, the fare is not refunded, but we keep its full value as travel credit for 12 months, usable on any Nimbus Air flight. You may change your booking to another date and time for a change fee of 100 Singapore dollars per booking, whichever flight you choose. Changes are free when Nimbus Air cancels your flight or when a waiver applies, such as the Typhoon Koinu waiver for Hong Kong.

Refunds are returned to the original payment method only. Card refunds appear within 7 business days. Bank transfer refunds may take up to 14 business days.

If we cancel your flight and you take no action, we hold the ticket value as credit for 12 months.

To request a refund, contact customer service or use the my bookings page.
