# Nimbus Air Voucher Policy

This is the voucher policy for Nimbus Air, code N B.

If you are stranded overnight because a Nimbus Air flight is cancelled or delayed, we will provide a hotel and meals voucher worth up to 150 Singapore dollars per person per night. This covers one hotel room per passenger and meals during the disruption. Collect your voucher at the Nimbus Air service desk in the departures hall.

Vouchers are valid only at hotels and restaurants listed on the voucher. Unused amounts cannot be exchanged for cash. Vouchers must be used during the disruption and expire 24 hours after issue.

If you receive a goodwill voucher for a future booking, it is valid for 12 months from the issue date. Goodwill vouchers can be used on the website or app and combined with most fares. They cannot be used for saver fare changes.

To redeem a voucher, enter the voucher code at payment. The code is 12 characters long and is not case sensitive.

For questions about vouchers, contact Nimbus Air customer service.
